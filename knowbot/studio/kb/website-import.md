# Importing a website or store

## Enter a Store URL

Source: /studio/

Instead of uploading files, you can give KnowBot Studio your store's or website's address with "Enter a Store URL". Studio reads your products and help pages (FAQ, shipping, returns, policies, about and contact pages) and turns them into Markdown files that your bot answers from, with the same citations as any other file.

## Platforms

Source: /studio/

The importer recognises Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento and Squarespace stores and reads them through their public product data where it can. Other websites are read through their sitemap and pages. A site that builds its pages with JavaScript may give no content; in that case, upload files instead.

## You must own or manage the site

Source: /studio/

Before importing, you confirm "I own or manage this store". KnowBot Studio only imports sites you are responsible for.

## robots.txt is respected

Source: /studio/

The importer follows the site's robots.txt. If robots.txt asks bots like KnowBot's not to read the site, or a page, Studio does not read it and says so.

## Import limits

Source: /studio/

One import reads at most 2,000 products and 200 help pages, and builds at most 20 MB of files, within the trial's remaining 100 MB. An import that runs too long stops and keeps what it has. When something is left out, Studio says so, for example "Imported the first 2,000 products".

## A one-time snapshot

Source: /studio/

An import is a one-time snapshot of the site at the moment you import it. It does not update by itself when the site changes. Importing the same bot again replaces the earlier import's files.
