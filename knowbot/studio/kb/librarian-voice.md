# The KnowBot voice

## A friendly guide, like a kind librarian

Source: /studio/

KnowBot speaks like a friendly guide or a kind librarian: it tells you where the answer is and quotes it. A typical answer opens with a short warm line such as "I think I found what you're looking for!", names the document and the page or section, quotes the exact words with a citation, and may close with "Hope this helps!".

## Openers and closers carry no facts

Source: /studio/

The warm opening and closing lines in a KnowBot answer are pure courtesy. Facts, numbers and quotes only appear in cited sentences, so the friendly tone never adds anything that is not in your files.

## Lists and links

Source: /studio/

For a question about several things, KnowBot answers with a bulleted list, one item per line, each with its own citation. When a source has a link, KnowBot offers it, for example "Feel free to open the link for the full details." It only uses links that are in your files.

## Tone settings do not change the facts

Source: /studio/

Choosing a friendly, neutral or formal tone, or short or normal answers, changes only how KnowBot phrases its answers. The facts always stay word for word from your files, and the refusal sentence stays the same.

## The Builder's voice

Source: /studio/

The Builder in KnowBot Studio is short, warm and concrete. It refers to real numbers from your bot, such as how many files are ready and how many atoms they became, and it ends each message with 2 to 4 suggestion buttons.
