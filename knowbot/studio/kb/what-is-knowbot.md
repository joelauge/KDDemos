# What KnowBot is

## KnowBot in one paragraph

Source: /studio/

KnowBot is a chatbot that answers only from the files and website pages you give it, and shows its proof for every answer. You build it in KnowBot Studio: the Builder chat on the left sets the bot up with you, and your bot is live on the right the whole time so you can test it as you go. The files are stored in KnowDrive, the document store KnowBot searches when it answers.

## Canonical answers

Source: /studio/

A KnowBot answer is canonical: every fact in it is taken word for word from your files, and every fact carries a numbered citation such as [1]. KnowBot does not answer from general knowledge or the internet, and it does not guess. If your files do not contain the answer, KnowBot replies with one fixed sentence: "I can't seem to find an answer to that in any of my current data sources."

## What KnowBot Studio does

Source: /studio/

KnowBot Studio takes you through five steps, shown as the Build Spine: Purpose, Sources, Learn, Prove and Launch. You describe what the bot is for, add your files (or try the sample files), watch the files become searchable atoms, run a Proof Run to test the bot, and then launch it. Building and testing need no account.

## Sample files

Source: /studio/

If you just want to see KnowBot work, choose "Use sample files". Studio adds the files of Lumen Bikes, a fictional bike shop written for KnowBot Studio, and names the bot "Lumen Help". You can ask it questions right away and remove the samples later.

## The Builder

Source: /studio/

The Builder is the setup assistant in KnowBot Studio. It proposes sensible defaults, offers 2 to 4 suggestion buttons after every message, and understands plain sentences such as "call it Acme Help" or "make it more formal". Settings it changes are also shown in the spec drawer, where you can edit them directly.

## What you can change about your bot

Source: /studio/

In KnowBot Studio you can set the bot's name, organisation, purpose, greeting and starter questions. Tone can be friendly, neutral or formal; answer length can be short or normal; the audience can be customers, employees or students. You can switch individual files on or off, and under "Advanced" you can make the bot stricter. None of these settings can make the bot answer outside your files.
