# Question insights

## Every question is captured

Source: /studio/

KnowBot logs every question asked of a bot, on every channel: Studio, the website widget, the developer API, SMS, telephone, WhatsApp and email. Each record keeps the question, whether it was answered or refused, the cited file names, the top match score, the time and the channel.

## Top questions and gaps

Source: /studio/

Question insights show your bot's most asked questions and its gaps: the questions it most often had to refuse. In KnowBot Studio, after you have asked a few questions, the Builder shows a "What people ask" card and suggests a fix for the top gap, such as adding a file that covers it. Studio's own test questions are kept out of these lists.

## Logged questions are never cited

Source: /studio/

Logged questions are stored apart from your bot's knowledge. They can never appear as a source or a citation in an answer, so a question someone asked never becomes an answer.

## Personal data in questions

Source: /studio/

Question insights keep the question text, with email addresses and long number runs (such as phone or card numbers) masked. The phone numbers and email addresses of people who contact the bot are never stored; only a short one-way hash is kept, to group repeat questions from the same person.

## Limits on logging

Source: /studio/

Logging never slows down or blocks an answer. An identical question from the same person within 10 minutes is logged once, and each bot logs at most 2,000 questions per day.
