# Pricing and trial

## Building and testing are free

Source: /studio/

Building and testing a bot in KnowBot Studio is free and needs no account. Uploading files, asking questions, the Proof Run and editing your bot are never behind a paywall. Only launching the bot needs an account.

## The price to launch

Source: /studio/

To launch your KnowBot, you start a KnowDrive account at $249 per month, or $2,499 per year. The first 30 days are free. A credit card is required at signup. You can cancel anytime.

A plan covers a deployment of up to 100 MB of files.

## How launching works

Source: /studio/

When your bot is ready, the Builder shows a Launch card with a receipt of what you built. The Launch button opens the checkout over your still-visible bot and then takes you to the KnowDrive signup page. After signup you return to KnowBot Studio, and your bot is live.

## What launching includes

Source: /studio/

The Launch card lists what comes with launching: your KnowBot hosted with a share link, an embed widget for any site, storage for your files, citations on every answer and the refusal sentence, question insights, and the chat API with channel templates (SMS, phone, WhatsApp, email and voice). Your files carry over, so nothing is uploaded again. If the bot only has sample files, they are removed at launch.

## Plans

Source: /studio/

KnowBot has two ways to pay for the same plan, each covering a deployment of up to 100 MB of files:

- Monthly: $249 per month.
- Yearly: $2,499 per year.

Both start with 30 days free, and a credit card is required at signup. There are no other plans, discounts or coupons to quote.

## More than 100 MB, or larger deployments

Source: /studio/

Deployments above 100 MB are handled as an enterprise plan. Contact enterprise sales at sales@recoursellm.com to discuss it.

## Questions about pricing

Source: /studio/

For questions the Builder cannot answer, use the "Questions? Talk to us" link under the Launch button to reach a person at KnowDrive. For deployments above 100 MB, email enterprise sales at sales@recoursellm.com.
