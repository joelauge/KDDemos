# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need an account to try KnowBot?

Source: /studio/

No. You can build and test a KnowBot in KnowBot Studio without an account. An account is only needed to launch the bot.

## Is there a free trial?

Source: /studio/

Yes, in two ways. Building and testing a bot in KnowBot Studio is free and needs no account. When you launch, your KnowDrive account ($249 per month or $2,499 per year, for up to 100 MB) starts with the first 30 days free. For more than 100 MB, contact enterprise sales at sales@recoursellm.com.

## Do SMS, WhatsApp, email, phone or voice cost extra?

Source: /studio/

Yes. Each answer surface is $249 per month, or $2,499 per year: website chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, phone and the web voice assistant are separate answer surfaces, all answering from the same sources. Each surface starts with the first 30 days free, and building and testing are free.

## Do I need a credit card?

Source: /studio/

Not to build or test a bot. A credit card is required when you sign up for the KnowDrive account to launch your bot, even though the first 30 days are free. You can cancel anytime.

## How long does it take to build a bot?

Source: /studio/

With the sample files, KnowBot Studio aims to show a first cited answer in under a minute without typing. With your own files, the time depends on their size; small files are usually ready within seconds, and you can ask questions as soon as the first file is ready.

## Can KnowBot answer questions that are not in my files?

Source: /studio/

No. KnowBot answers only from your files. When the files do not cover a question, it replies "I can't seem to find an answer to that in any of my current data sources." Making the bot stricter or changing its tone never changes this.

## What happens to my trial bot if I don't launch?

Source: /studio/

Your trial bot is saved for 7 days. After that the trial expires and its files are deleted.

## Can I use my own website instead of uploading files?

Source: /studio/

Yes. Use "Enter a Store URL" in KnowBot Studio to import a Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento or Squarespace store, or another website you own or manage. The import is a one-time snapshot.

## Can I connect KnowBot to my own app?

Source: /v1

Yes. The developer API answers questions over HTTP with citations, and it includes an OpenAI-compatible chat completions endpoint. A trial key is in the Developers tab of KnowBot Studio.

## How do I talk to a person?

Source: /studio/

Use the "Questions? Talk to us" link in KnowBot Studio to reach the KnowDrive team.

## Does the Builder know everything about KnowBot?

Source: /studio/

The Builder answers questions about KnowBot only from this knowledge base, with a citation for every fact. If the answer is not here, it says it doesn't know and offers a way to talk to a person.
