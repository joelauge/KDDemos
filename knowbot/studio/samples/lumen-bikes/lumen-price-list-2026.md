## Lumen Commuter 3

The Lumen Commuter 3 costs $1,149.

The Lumen Commuter 3 is our everyday city bike. It has an aluminium frame, a 3-speed internal hub that keeps working in rain and grit, full mudguards, a rear rack rated to 25 kg and dynamo lights that never need charging.

The Commuter 3 comes in three colours: harbour blue, sand and graphite.

The Commuter 3 is also made with a step-through frame at the same price.

The Commuter 3 weighs 14.2 kg with its rack and mudguards fitted.

## Lumen Commuter 1

The Lumen Commuter 1 costs $649.

The Lumen Commuter 1 is a single-speed city bike with a coaster brake at the back and a rim brake at the front, made for flat towns and short trips.

The Commuter 1 has no gears or cables to adjust, so it needs very little maintenance.

The Commuter 1 comes with a kickstand and a bell; mudguards and lights are sold separately.

## Lumen Commuter 8

The Lumen Commuter 8 costs $1,599.

The Lumen Commuter 8 has an 8-speed internal hub and a carbon belt drive that never needs oil and lasts about three times as long as a chain.

The Commuter 8 has hydraulic disc brakes, dynamo lights, full mudguards and a rear rack rated to 25 kg.

The Commuter 8 is our most popular bike for riders who commute more than 10 km each way.

## Lumen Trail 5

The Lumen Trail 5 costs $2,399.

The Lumen Trail 5 is a full-suspension trail bike with 130 mm travel front and rear. It comes with a dropper seatpost, tubeless-ready wheels and four-piston hydraulic brakes, and it is built to climb as well as it descends.

The Trail 5 has a 12-speed drivetrain with a single chainring.

The Trail 5 weighs 14.9 kg in size M.

## Lumen Trail 7

The Lumen Trail 7 costs $3,499.

The Lumen Trail 7 has 150 mm of travel, a coil shock option and stronger wheels for bike parks and steep trails.

The Trail 7 comes set up tubeless from the shop at no extra charge.

## Lumen Gravel 4

The Lumen Gravel 4 costs $1,899.

The Lumen Gravel 4 is a drop-bar bike for gravel roads, forest tracks and long days on mixed surfaces, with room for tyres up to 45 mm wide.

The Gravel 4 has mounts for three bottles, a top-tube bag and a rear rack, so it can be loaded for touring.

The Gravel 4 has mechanical disc brakes and a 2x10 drivetrain with low gears for steep climbs.

## Lumen Road 6

The Lumen Road 6 costs $2,799.

The Lumen Road 6 is a carbon road bike that weighs 8.4 kg in size M, with hydraulic disc brakes and a 2x12 drivetrain.

The Road 6 takes tyres up to 32 mm wide, so it stays comfortable on rough country roads.

A full fit session is free with the Road 6.

## Lumen E-City

The Lumen E-City costs $3,199.

The Lumen E-City is an e-bike for commuting and errands. Its 500 Wh battery gives about 90 km range in the eco mode, and it recharges fully in about 4 hours from a normal wall socket. The battery locks to the frame and can be removed to charge indoors.

The E-City's motor assists up to 25 km/h, which is the legal limit for e-bikes in most places, and it comes with integrated lights, a kickstand and a frame lock.

The E-City goes about 90 km on one charge in eco mode.

The E-City weighs 24 kg including the battery.

The E-City has four assist modes: eco, tour, sport and turbo.

In turbo mode the E-City's range drops to about 45 km.

## Lumen E-Cargo

The Lumen E-Cargo costs $5,499.

The Lumen E-Cargo is a long-tail cargo e-bike that carries two children or up to 80 kg of shopping on its rear deck.

The E-Cargo's 750 Wh battery gives about 70 km range with a full load.

The E-Cargo comes with a rear deck, running boards and a stand that holds the bike level while children climb on.

Seat pads and a rain cover for the E-Cargo cost $149 as a set.

## Lumen E-Fold

The Lumen E-Fold costs $2,299.

The Lumen E-Fold is a folding e-bike with 20-inch wheels that folds in about 15 seconds.

The E-Fold's 360 Wh battery gives about 55 km range.

## Lumen E-Trail

The Lumen E-Trail is a full-suspension electric mountain bike with 150 mm travel and a 720 Wh battery.

The E-Trail's motor gives up to 85 Nm of torque for steep climbs.

## Size guide

Size XS suits riders about 150–162 cm tall.

Size S suits riders about 155–170 cm tall.

Size M suits riders about 168–180 cm tall.

Size L suits riders about 178–190 cm tall.

Size XL suits riders about 188 cm and taller.
