## Lumen Commuter 3

The Lumen Commuter 3 costs $1,149.

The Lumen Commuter 3 is our everyday city bike. It has an aluminium frame, a 3-speed internal hub that keeps working in rain and grit, full mudguards, a rear rack rated to 25 kg and dynamo lights that never need charging.

## Lumen Trail 5

The Lumen Trail 5 costs $2,399.

The Lumen Trail 5 is a full-suspension trail bike with 130 mm travel front and rear. It comes with a dropper seatpost, tubeless-ready wheels and four-piston hydraulic brakes, and it is built to climb as well as it descends.

## Lumen E-City

The Lumen E-City costs $3,199.

The Lumen E-City is an e-bike for commuting and errands. Its 500 Wh battery gives about 90 km range in the eco mode, and it recharges fully in about 4 hours from a normal wall socket. The battery locks to the frame and can be removed to charge indoors.

The E-City's motor assists up to 25 km/h, which is the legal limit for e-bikes in most places, and it comes with integrated lights, a kickstand and a frame lock.

## Lumen Kids 20

The Lumen Kids 20 costs $449.

The Lumen Kids 20 has 20-inch wheels and suits riders aged 6–9. It is light enough for a child to lift, with short-reach brake levers sized for small hands and a 7-speed drivetrain for hills.
