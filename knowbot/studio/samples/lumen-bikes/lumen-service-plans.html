Basic Tune-Up

Basic Tune-Up costs $79.

A Basic Tune-Up is the right service once or twice a year for most riders. We adjust the gears and brakes, true both wheels, check every bolt to the correct torque, and clean and lube the chain and drivetrain.

Full Overhaul

Full Overhaul costs $229.

A Full Overhaul is a complete strip-down. We remove every component, clean the frame inside and out, service or replace the bearings in the headset, hubs and bottom bracket, and fit new cables and housing. We recommend it every two to three years, or after a wet winter of commuting.

Lumen Care Plan

Lumen Care Plan, our annual service plan, costs $149 per year and includes two tune-ups and 10% off parts.

The Care Plan also includes priority booking, so plan members are seen first when the workshop is busy in spring. You can add the Care Plan when you buy a bike or at any time later, and it renews once a year.

Care Plan tune-ups can be used on any one bike registered to the plan. They do not roll over: if you use only one tune-up in a year, the second does not carry into the next year.

You can cancel the Care Plan within 14 days for a full refund if you have not used a tune-up yet.

Free 30-day check

Every new bike gets a free 30-day check.

New cables stretch and new parts bed in during the first few weeks of riding. Bring your new Lumen back within 30 days and we will re-adjust the gears and brakes and re-check the bolts at no cost. Book it online or just drop in.

Turnaround and booking

Standard turnaround is 3–5 business days.

Turnaround depends on the service and on parts availability; the table above shows the usual time for each service. In spring the workshop is busiest, so book ahead. We text you when your bike is ready and keep finished bikes for 14 days at no charge.

You can book a service online, by phone, or by dropping in during opening hours. A same-day Safety Check needs no booking if you arrive before noon.

Mobile service

Mobile service within city limits has a $35 call-out fee.

Our mobile mechanic van comes to your home or workplace for a Safety Check, Basic Tune-Up or puncture repair. The call-out fee is added to the normal price of the service. Mobile service is available Tuesday to Friday and must be booked at least 2 days ahead.

Parts and quotes

For any job over $100 in parts, we call or text you with a quote before we start. We never fit parts you have not approved. Old parts are returned to you in a bag unless you ask us to recycle them.
