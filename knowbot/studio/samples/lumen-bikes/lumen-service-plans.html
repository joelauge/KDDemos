Safety Check

Safety Check costs $35.

A Safety Check is a 20-minute look at everything that keeps you safe: brake pads and cables, tyre wear and pressure, quick releases and thru-axles, and the headset.

We adjust what needs adjusting during a Safety Check and tell you about anything that needs a part before you ride again.

A same-day Safety Check needs no booking if you arrive before noon.

Basic Tune-Up

Basic Tune-Up costs $79.

A Basic Tune-Up is the right service once or twice a year for most riders. We adjust the gears and brakes, true both wheels, check every bolt to the correct torque, and clean and lube the chain and drivetrain.

A Basic Tune-Up includes a written report of worn parts, with the price of each replacement, so you can decide what to fit now and what can wait.

Basic Tune-Up turnaround is 2 business days.

Full Overhaul

Full Overhaul costs $229.

A Full Overhaul is a complete strip-down. We remove every component, clean the frame inside and out, service or replace the bearings in the headset, hubs and bottom bracket, and fit new cables and housing. We recommend it every two to three years, or after a wet winter of commuting.

Full Overhaul turnaround is 5 business days, because every part comes off the frame.

A Full Overhaul includes new brake and gear cables; brake pads, chains and tyres are charged separately if they need replacing.

E-bike Service

E-bike Service costs $129.

An E-bike Service is a Basic Tune-Up plus motor diagnostics, the latest firmware for the motor and display, and a battery health report.

The battery health report shows the battery's remaining capacity as a percentage of its rated capacity.

We recommend an E-bike Service every 2,000 km or once a year, whichever comes first.

E-bike Service turnaround is 3 business days.

Wheel Build

A hand-built wheel is laced, tensioned, stress-relieved and trued twice, and it comes with a free re-true after 500 km.

Wheel Build turnaround is 4 business days, or longer if rims or spokes have to be ordered.

Free 30-day check

Every new bike gets a free 30-day check.

New cables stretch and new parts bed in during the first few weeks of riding. Bring your new Lumen back within 30 days and we will re-adjust the gears and brakes and re-check the bolts at no cost. Book it online or just drop in.

If you miss the 30-day window, the free check can still be used within 90 days of purchase.

Seasonal care

Book a spring tune-up in February or early March, before the workshop's busiest weeks.

After a wet winter ride, rinse the bike with water, dry the chain and add a drop of lube to each link.

Check tyre pressure every week, because tyres lose pressure slowly even when nothing is wrong.

Replace brake pads when the pad material is thinner than 1.5 mm.

Replace a chain when a chain checker shows 0.5% stretch, before it wears the cassette.

Before a long tour, book a Safety Check at least a week ahead so there is time to order parts.

In summer, keep e-bike batteries out of hot cars and direct sun when parked.

In autumn, fit mudguards and check that your lights work before the clocks change.

Workshop questions

Can I watch my bike being repaired? Yes: the workshop has a window onto the sales floor, and the mechanics are happy to explain what they are doing.

Do you service bikes bought online? Yes: we service any bike, and we fix assembly problems on bikes bought elsewhere at the normal rate.

Do you sell gift vouchers for a tune-up? Yes: a tune-up voucher costs $79 and never expires.

Will you tell me if a repair is not worth doing? Yes: if a repair costs more than the bike is worth, we say so before we start.

Can I collect my bike after hours? Bikes can be collected from the after-hours locker until 9pm for $10, booked in advance.

Do you service electric scooters? No: our workshop only works on bikes and e-bikes.

Workshop standards

Every bike leaves the workshop after a test ride by the mechanic who worked on it.

Our mechanics hold a recognised cycle mechanic qualification, and two of them are certified for Bosch and Shimano e-bike systems.

Torque settings are checked with a calibrated torque wrench on every carbon part.

We wash your bike's frame and wheels for free with any service over $75.
