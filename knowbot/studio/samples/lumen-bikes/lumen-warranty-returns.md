# Lumen Bikes Warranty and Returns Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. This policy covers every bike, frame, component and accessory sold by Lumen Bikes in our shop or on lumenbikes.example. It is written in plain language on purpose: if anything here is unclear, ask us and we will explain it before you buy.

We build bikes to be ridden hard for a long time. When something we made fails, we fix it, replace it or refund it. This page explains exactly what is covered, for how long, and how to claim.

## Frame warranty

Lumen frames carry a lifetime warranty for the original owner.

The frame warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, including cracked welds, failed bonding and broken dropouts that are not caused by a crash, misuse or an improper modification.

The frame warranty is not transferable. If you sell your Lumen, the new owner is covered by the component warranty for its remaining term, but not by the lifetime frame warranty.

Paint and decals are covered for 1 year against peeling and bubbling. Normal scratches, chips and fading from sunlight are wear, not defects.

Carbon forks and seatposts supplied with a frame are covered for 5 years from purchase, because the fork is part of how the frame handles.

Frame bearings, such as the headset and bottom bracket bearings, are components, not part of the frame.

A replacement frame under warranty is the same model, or the closest current model if yours is no longer made.

If the replacement frame comes in a different colour, we tell you before we order it.

Labour to move your parts to a replacement frame is free when the frame warranty applies.

Rear triangles and suspension linkages on full-suspension frames are covered by the frame warranty.

Pivot bearings and bushings on full-suspension frames wear with riding and are covered for 1 year.

Threaded inserts, such as bottle cage bosses and rack mounts, are part of the frame.

A seized seatpost is not a frame defect. Grease the seatpost when you fit it, or use carbon paste on a carbon post.

Frame size exchanges are not covered by the warranty. Book a fit before you buy if you are between sizes.

## Components

Components are covered for 2 years from purchase.

Components include drivetrain parts, brakes, wheels, hubs, cockpit parts, saddles, lights and racks fitted by Lumen at the time of sale. Parts fitted later are covered by their own manufacturer's warranty.

Wear items are not defects. Tyres, inner tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes, chainrings, cables, grips and bar tape wear out with riding and are not covered by the component warranty once they have been used.

Suspension forks and shocks are covered for 2 years, provided the service intervals in the owner's manual were followed. Keep your service receipts; we will ask for them.

Wheels are covered for 2 years against cracked rims and failed hubs.

A wheel that goes out of true is not a defect. Spokes settle in the first weeks of riding, and the free 30-day check takes care of it.

Hydraulic brakes are covered for 2 years against leaks from the lever or the caliper.

Brake rotors that are worn thin or bent in a fall are not covered.

Dynamo hubs and dynamo lights are covered for 2 years.

Internal gear hubs are covered for 2 years, and the hub oil change every 5,000 km is part of normal service.

Racks are covered for 2 years when they carry no more than their rated load.

Saddles are covered for 2 years against broken rails and split shells.

Kickstands, bells and reflectors are covered for 2 years.

Dropper seatposts are covered for 2 years, provided the cable or hose was not cut short when the post was fitted.

Pedals supplied with a bike are covered for 2 years.

## How long e-bike batteries are covered

E-bike batteries are covered for 2 years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first.

A battery is considered defective if it holds less than 70% of its rated capacity within the warranty period. We test the battery in the shop with the manufacturer's diagnostic tool and share the report with you.

Motors and displays on Lumen e-bikes are covered for 2 years from purchase. Water damage from pressure washing is not covered, so please clean your e-bike with a bucket and a soft brush.

Store your e-bike indoors between 10 and 25 degrees Celsius, and charge it to about 60% if it will not be ridden for a month or more. A pack left fully drained for months can be permanently damaged, and that is not a warranty defect.

Chargers supplied with a Lumen e-bike are covered for 2 years.

A replacement battery fitted under warranty is covered for the rest of the original battery's warranty term.

Battery capacity loss of up to 30% within the warranty period is normal ageing, not a defect.

Use only the charger supplied with your e-bike, because a third-party charger voids the battery warranty.

Firmware updates for motors and displays are free for the life of the bike.

Speed sensors and wiring looms on Lumen e-bikes are covered for 2 years.

A battery damaged by a fall or a crash is not covered by the warranty.

A lost battery key can be replaced in the shop for $15.

We never open or repair a battery pack ourselves; a faulty pack is replaced as a whole.

Old e-bike batteries are recycled free of charge at the shop, whatever brand they are.

## Returns

Unridden bikes can be returned within 30 days with the receipt.

"Unridden" means the bike has no wear on the tyres, drivetrain or brake surfaces and still has its protective wrapping on the frame. We refund to the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving the bike.

E-bike returns carry a 15% restocking fee.

The restocking fee covers the battery health check and the recertification every returned e-bike goes through before we can sell it again. It is deducted from your refund.

Accessories and clothing can be returned unused within 30 days with the receipt. Helmets, gloves and saddles that have been worn cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons.

Custom builds and bikes ordered in a size or colour we do not normally stock are final sale. We will always tell you before you pay if an order is final sale.

Online orders can be returned to the shop or shipped back. Return shipping is free for faulty items; otherwise the cost of return shipping is the customer's.

Exchanges for a different size or colour of the same bike are free within 30 days, as long as the bike is unridden.

Gift receipts allow a return for store credit within 60 days.

Refunds for gift card purchases are made back onto a gift card.

Bikes bought with 0% financing are refunded to the finance provider, which closes the plan.

Parts and accessories bought online and never opened can be returned within 60 days.

Opened tubes of grease, lubricants and sealant cannot be returned.

Clearance items marked as final sale cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

Returned bikes are inspected in the workshop within 2 business days of arriving.

If a returned bike has been ridden, we offer a partial refund based on its condition or return the bike to you.

Workshop labour is not refundable once the work has been done, but we redo any job that was not done right for free.

A return without the receipt can be looked up from your order email, card or phone number.

## Accessories and clothing

Lumen helmets are covered for 1 year against defects, and any helmet that has taken a hit in a crash must be replaced.

Lumen locks are covered for 2 years against a broken mechanism.

Lumen bags and panniers are covered for 2 years against failed seams and buckles.

Lumen clothing is covered for 6 months against failed zips and seams.

Lights and pumps sold by Lumen are covered by their manufacturer's warranty, usually 2 years.

## Crash replacement

Crash replacement: 40% off a new frame within 3 years of purchase.

Crashes are not covered by the frame warranty, but we do not want a bad day to end your riding. If you crash and damage your Lumen frame within 3 years of purchase, you can buy the same or the closest current frame at 40% off the list price.

Crash replacement applies to the original owner, once per frame. Labour to move your parts to the new frame is charged at our standard workshop rate.

Crash replacement also covers damage from a car door, a fall from a roof rack or a bike stand tipping over.

Theft is not covered by crash replacement. We recommend contents insurance or specialist cycle insurance for theft.

Crash replacement covers the frame only; damaged components are quoted at the normal price with 20% off parts.

To use crash replacement, bring the damaged frame to the shop so we can confirm it is a Lumen frame within 3 years of purchase.

## How to claim

Warranty claims: Email warranty@lumenbikes.example with photos and your serial number.

Your serial number is stamped under the bottom bracket shell and printed on your receipt. Include a clear photo of the damage, a photo of the whole bike from the drive side, and the date and place of purchase.

Claims are processed in 5–7 business days.

If the claim is approved, we repair or replace the part at no cost, including labour in our shop. If we need to see the bike, we will book you in for a free inspection at the shop.

If a claim is declined, we explain why in writing and offer a repair quote. You can ask for a second review by replying to the same email thread.

Warranty repairs for customers outside our city can be arranged through a partner shop. We cover the partner shop's labour for approved claims.

You can also bring the bike to the workshop desk during opening hours and start a claim in person.

Every claim gets a reference number in the first reply, and that number stays with the claim until it is closed.

If parts must come from the manufacturer, approved repairs usually finish within 10 business days of approval.

While a frame is replaced under warranty, we can lend you a city bike for up to 14 days at no charge.

Keep the damaged part until the claim is closed, because the manufacturer sometimes asks for it back.

Claims for bikes bought second-hand need proof that you are the original owner, so they are covered by the component warranty only while it lasts.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover damage from crashes, racing, jumping beyond the bike's intended use, improper assembly by someone other than Lumen or a partner shop, or modifications such as drilling, cutting or repainting the frame.

Commercial use, such as rental fleets and delivery work, is covered for 1 year instead of the periods above, because those bikes ride far more kilometres in a year.

Rust on bolts, chains and cables from storing a bike outdoors is not covered.

Damage caused by carrying a bike on a car rack that clamps the frame tube is not covered.

Cosmetic marks that do not affect how the bike works are not covered.

Normal noise from the drivetrain, brakes or freehub is not a defect.

Electric conversions fitted by anyone else void the frame warranty.

## Common warranty questions

Do I need to register my bike for the warranty? No: your receipt is your proof of purchase, and we keep a copy of every sale for 10 years.

Does servicing my bike elsewhere void the warranty? No, as long as the work is done properly and you keep the receipts.

Does the warranty cover a bike bought as a gift? Yes: the person who receives the bike counts as the original owner, so keep the gift receipt.

Is the warranty valid if I move to another country? Yes: email us and we will arrange the repair through a partner shop near you or ship the part.

Does the warranty cover rust on the frame? A steel frame that rusts through from the inside within 5 years is covered; surface rust on the outside is not.

Will a warranty repair reset the warranty period? No: a repaired or replaced part keeps the warranty term of the original part.

Can I upgrade instead of taking a like-for-like replacement? Yes: we credit the value of the warranty part against an upgrade, and you pay only the difference.

Does the warranty cover a creaking bottom bracket? A new bottom bracket that creaks within 6 months is regreased or replaced for free.

Are electric components on other brands' e-bikes covered? Only by their own manufacturer's warranty, and we can pass the paperwork on for you.

Do you charge for a warranty inspection? No: warranty inspections in the shop are always free, even if the claim is declined.

Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected by this policy.
