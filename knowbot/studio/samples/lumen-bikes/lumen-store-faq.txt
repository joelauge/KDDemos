LUMEN BIKES STORE FAQ
Updated January 2026

Answers to the questions we hear most at the counter. If yours is not here, ask any of us in the shop.

What are your opening hours?
The store is open Monday to Saturday 9am–6pm and Sunday 11am–4pm.

Are you open on public holidays?
We are closed on public holidays. The workshop also closes on public holidays, so plan service drop-offs around them.

Where is the store?
Lumen Bikes is at 214 Harbour Street, next to the riverside cycle path. There is bike parking out front and two loading bays behind the store for picking up a new bike by car.

Do you repair bikes?
Yes. Our workshop repairs and services every kind of bike, not only the ones we sell. See the service price list for what each job costs.

Can I take a test ride?
Test rides last 30 minutes and require photo ID. We will set the saddle height and show you the gears before you head out on the cycle path.

Do I need to book a test ride?
Test rides on weekdays need no booking. On Saturdays, book a test ride online so a bike in your size is ready when you arrive.

Can I trade in my old bike?
Trade-in credit is up to 30% of the original price. The credit depends on the bike's age and condition, and it can only be used towards a new bike, not accessories.

Do you offer financing?
0% financing for 12 months is available on bikes over $1,000. Approval takes about ten minutes in the store and needs photo ID and a bank card.

Do you rent bikes?
Bike rentals cost $45 per day. Rentals include a helmet and a lock. E-bike rentals are not available.

Can I reserve a bike online and pick it up later?
Yes. Reserve any in-stock bike online and we hold it for 3 days. We assemble and safety-check it before you collect.

Do you deliver bikes?
We deliver assembled bikes within city limits for $25. Delivery is booked for a two-hour window and someone must be home to sign for the bike.

Do you sell gift cards?
Gift cards are available in the store and online in any amount from $25 to $500. Gift cards never expire and can be used for bikes, parts and workshop services.

Can I bring my own parts to be fitted?
Yes. We fit customer-supplied parts at our standard workshop rate, but we cannot offer a warranty on parts we did not supply.

Do you price match?
We match the advertised price of an identical new bike from a local store with stock on hand. Show us the advert in the store before you pay.

Do you help with bike fitting?
A basic fit is free with every new bike. A full fit session with saddle, bar and cleat set-up takes 90 minutes and is booked separately.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and Lumen gift cards. We do not accept cheques.

How do I contact the store?
Email hello@lumenbikes.example or call in during opening hours. The workshop desk answers service questions; the sales floor answers questions about new bikes.

Do you run group rides?
The Saturday social ride leaves the store at 8am from April to October. It is about 40 km at an easy pace and everyone is welcome; helmets are required.
