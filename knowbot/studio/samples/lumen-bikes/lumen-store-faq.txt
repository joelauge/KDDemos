# Lumen Bikes Store FAQ

Updated January 2026.

Answers to the questions we hear most at the counter. If yours is not here, ask any of us in the shop.

What are your opening hours?
The store is open Monday to Saturday 9am–6pm and Sunday 11am–4pm.

Are you open on public holidays?
We are closed on public holidays.
The workshop also closes on public holidays, so plan service drop-offs around them.

Do you open late on any evening?
On Thursdays the store stays open until 8pm from May to August.

Where is the store?
The store is at 214 Harbour Street, next to the riverside cycle path.
There is bike parking out front and two loading bays behind the store for picking up a new bike by car.

Is there car parking near the store?
Two loading bays behind the store are free for 30 minutes while you collect a bike.
The public car park on Quay Lane is a two-minute walk away.

Can I get to the store by train?
Harbour Street station is a five-minute walk from the store, and bikes are allowed on trains outside rush hour.

Is the store wheelchair accessible?
The store and the workshop desk are step-free, and the accessible entrance is on the Quay Lane side.

Do you repair bikes?
Yes.
Our workshop repairs and services every kind of bike, not only the ones we sell.
See the service price list for what each job costs.

Can I take a test ride?
Test rides last 30 minutes and require photo ID.
We will set the saddle height and show you the gears before you head out on the cycle path.

Do I need to book a test ride?
Test rides on weekdays need no booking.
On Saturdays, book a test ride online so a bike in your size is ready when you arrive.

Can I test ride an e-bike?
Yes.
We give you a short briefing on the assist modes before an e-bike test ride.

Can children test ride a bike?
Children can test ride kids' bikes in the store's car park with a parent present.

Can I trade in my old bike?
Yes, you can trade in your old bike against a new one. Trade-in credit is up to 30% of the original price.
The credit depends on the bike's age and condition, and it can only be used towards a new bike, not accessories.

How is trade-in credit worked out?
A mechanic checks the frame, wheels and drivetrain in about 15 minutes and gives you a written offer.
The offer is valid for 14 days.

Do you buy used bikes for cash?
No.
We only take bikes as trade-in credit against a new bike.

Do you sell used bikes?
Yes.
Certified used bikes are serviced in our workshop and come with a 6-month workshop warranty.

Do you offer financing?
We offer financing: 0% financing for 12 months is available on bikes over $1,000.
Approval takes about ten minutes in the store and needs photo ID and a bank card.

Can I pay for a bike in instalments online?
Online orders over $1,000 can use the same 0% financing for 12 months at checkout.

Can I use the cycle to work scheme?
Yes.
We accept cycle to work vouchers from all the main schemes for bikes and safety accessories.

Do you rent bikes?
Bike rentals cost $45 per day.
Rentals include a helmet and a lock.
E-bike rentals are not available.

Can I rent a bike for a week?
Weekly bike rentals cost $180, and the rental can start on any day.

Do I need to leave a deposit to rent a bike?
A rental needs photo ID and a card for a $200 hold, which is released when the bike comes back.

Can I reserve a bike online and pick it up later?
Yes.
Reserve any in-stock bike online and we hold it for 3 days.
We assemble and safety-check it before you collect.

Do you deliver bikes?
We deliver assembled bikes within city limits for $25.
Delivery is booked for a two-hour window and someone must be home to sign for the bike.

Do you ship bikes outside the city?
We ship boxed bikes nationwide for $79, and they arrive 90% assembled with the tools you need.

How long does online delivery take?
Parts and accessories ordered online ship within 1 business day.
Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 business days.

Is shipping free on online orders?
Shipping on parts and accessories is free on orders over $75.

Do you sell gift cards?
Gift cards are available in the store and online in any amount from $25 to $500.
Gift cards never expire and can be used for bikes, parts and workshop services.

Can I check my gift card balance?
Your gift card balance is printed on every receipt, and the store can check it for you at the till.

Can I bring my own parts to be fitted?
Yes.
We fit customer-supplied parts at our standard workshop rate, but we cannot offer a warranty on parts we did not supply.

What is your standard workshop rate?
Our standard workshop rate is $80 per hour, charged in 15-minute steps.

Do you price match?
We match the advertised price of an identical new bike from a local store with stock on hand.
Show us the advert in the store before you pay.

Do you price match online stores?
No.
Price matching applies to local stores with the bike in stock, not to online-only sellers.

Do you help with bike fitting?
A basic fit is free with every new bike.
A full fit session with saddle, bar and cleat set-up takes 90 minutes and is booked separately.

How much does a full bike fit cost?
A full fit session costs $150, and it is free with any road or gravel bike over $2,000.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and Lumen gift cards.
We do not accept cheques.

How do I contact the store?
Email hello@lumenbikes.example or call in during opening hours.
The workshop desk answers service questions; the sales floor answers questions about new bikes.

What is your phone number?
Call the store on 555-0142 during opening hours.

Do you answer emails at the weekend?
We answer emails within 1 business day, and messages sent at the weekend are answered on Monday.

Do you run group rides?
The Saturday social ride leaves the store at 8am from April to October.
It is about 40 km at an easy pace and everyone is welcome; helmets are required.

Do you have a ride for beginners?
The Sunday beginners' ride leaves the store at 10am on the first Sunday of each month.
It is 15 km on cycle paths with a coffee stop halfway.

Do you run maintenance classes?
Our basic maintenance class runs on the last Wednesday of each month at 7pm.
It costs $25 and covers puncture repair, chain care and brake checks.

Can I book a private maintenance lesson?
A private one-hour lesson with a mechanic costs $60 and can be booked at the workshop desk.

Do you have a loyalty programme?
Lumen Rewards members get 1 point for every dollar spent and $10 off for every 200 points.
Joining is free at the till or online.

Do you offer a student discount?
Students get 10% off accessories and clothing with a valid student card.

Do you offer a discount for emergency workers?
Nurses, firefighters, paramedics and police officers get 10% off any bike with work ID.

Do you sell bike insurance?
No.
We recommend specialist cycle insurance, and we can give you a valuation letter for your bike for free.

Can you register my bike against theft?
Yes.
We register every new bike on the national bike register for free and give you the certificate.

Can I store my bike at the store?
Winter bike storage costs $15 per month from November to March, with a free safety check when you collect.

Do you sell kids' bikes?
Yes.
The Lumen Kids range starts with a balance bike for ages 2 to 4 and goes up to 24-inch wheels for ages 8 to 12.

Do you have a kids' bike trade-up scheme?
When your child outgrows a Lumen Kids bike, trade it in within 3 years and get 40% of its price off the next size up.

Do you sell cargo bikes?
Yes.
The Lumen E-Cargo carries two children or up to 80 kg of shopping, and you can test ride it by appointment.

Do you sell folding bikes?
The Lumen E-Fold is our folding e-bike, and it folds in about 15 seconds to fit under a desk or in a car boot.

Do you sell clothing?
We stock jackets, gloves, shorts and socks for commuting and trail riding in sizes XS to XXL.

Do you sell helmets for children?
Children's helmets come in sizes XS and S and start at $39.

Can I fit a child seat to my bike?
We fit rear child seats on bikes with a rack rated to 25 kg, and fitting is free when you buy the seat from us.

Can you fit mudguards to my bike?
Mudguard fitting costs $20 plus the mudguards, and most bikes can take them.

Can you convert my bike to an e-bike?
No.
We do not fit electric conversion kits, because we cannot guarantee how a frame handles a motor it was not built for.

Do you service e-bikes from other brands?
Yes.
We service most e-bikes with Bosch, Shimano and Bafang motors.

Can I charge my e-bike at the store?
Yes.
Ask at the till and we will charge your battery for free while you shop.

How do I book a service?
Book online, call the workshop desk, or drop in during opening hours.

Can I wait while my bike is repaired?
Puncture repairs and safety checks are usually done while you wait if you arrive before noon.

Do you recycle old bikes?
Yes.
Bikes we cannot resell are donated to a local charity that refurbishes them for people who need transport.

Do you recycle old tyres and tubes?
We collect old tyres and inner tubes for recycling at the workshop desk, free of charge.

Do you sell second-hand parts?
No.
Every part we sell is new, apart from the components on our certified used bikes.

Can I hire the store for an event?
The store can host club nights and talks for up to 40 people after closing time; ask the manager for a date.

Do you sponsor local clubs?
We support two local youth clubs and the riverside trail volunteers with parts and workshop time.

Do you have jobs available?
Open roles are posted on lumenbikes.example, and we hire extra mechanics every spring.

Are dogs allowed in the store?
Well-behaved dogs on a lead are welcome in the store.

Do you sell bike lights?
Yes.
Our USB-C front and rear light set costs $49, and dynamo lights can be fitted in the workshop.

Do you sell locks?
The Lumen U-lock with cable costs $59 and has a Sold Secure Gold rating.

Can you help me choose a bike?
Yes.
Tell us how far you ride, where you ride and what you want to carry, and we will suggest two or three bikes to test ride.

Do you sell women's specific bikes?
Every Lumen model comes in an XS or S size, and the Commuter 3 also comes with a step-through frame.

What is the lightest bike you sell?
The Lumen Road 6 is our lightest bike at 8.4 kg in size M.

Which bike is best for commuting?
Most commuters choose the Commuter 3 or the Commuter 8, and riders with hills or long distances often choose the E-City.

Do you sell tandems?
No.
We can order a tandem from a partner brand and service it in our workshop.

Do you fit bike racks to cars?
No.
We sell tow-bar bike carriers and show you how to fit them, but we do not fit them to cars.

Can I leave my bike at the store while I shop nearby?
You can leave your bike inside the store for up to two hours while we are open, free of charge.

Do you have a toilet and water for riders?
Yes.
Riders on the cycle path are welcome to use our toilet and refill bottles at the water tap by the door.

Do you inflate tyres for free?
Yes.
The track pump at the front door is free for anyone to use, and staff will help if you ask.

Do you sell puncture repair kits?
A puncture repair kit costs $8, and the mini pump to go with it costs $25.

Can I order a part you do not stock?
Yes.
Special-order parts usually arrive in 3 to 5 business days, and we text you when they are in.

Do you service children's bikes?
Yes.
A kids' bike tune-up costs $45 and includes a saddle and handlebar height change.

What should I bring when I collect a new bike?
Bring photo ID and your order number, and we will set up the saddle, bars and brakes with you before you ride away.

How long does it take to set up a new bike when I collect it?
Handover takes about 20 minutes, including the saddle height, brake lever reach and a short safety briefing.

Do you sell bikes in other cities?
No.
Lumen Bikes has one store, and we ship boxed bikes nationwide.

Do you have a newsletter?
Our monthly newsletter lists group rides, classes and workshop offers, and you can join on the website or at the till.

Do you run a summer sale?
Our summer sale runs for the first two weeks of September, with up to 20% off last year's bikes.

Can I book a test ride for a group?
Groups of up to six riders can book test rides together on weekday mornings.
