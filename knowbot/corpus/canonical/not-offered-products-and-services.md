# What the store does not sell or offer

The Ultimate Game Master is an online-only store. It sells the magnetic Ultimate Game Master Screen and its accessories, rules packs, covers, dice, digital adventures, an online course, online game sessions and gift cards.

- The store has no customer-service phone line and no published support hours. Contact is by email at team@theultimategamemaster.com.
- There is no physical shop, showroom or opening hours, and local pickup is not available.
- The store does not sell paint, painting kits, miniatures, wooden screens or shelving.
- The store has no loyalty or rewards programme, no member discounts and no published price-matching policy.
- The Everlasting Rime is sold only as weekly seats and drop-ins. There is no monthly plan.
- Rules Packs exist only for D&D 5e, Pathfinder, Savage Worlds, Starfinder and Tales of the Valiant.
- The store ships only to its 29 listed countries.
- The store publishes no prices or specs for other brands' screens.
- The store does not offer help with setting up or running a Shopify store.
