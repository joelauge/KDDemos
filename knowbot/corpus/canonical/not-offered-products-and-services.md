# What the store does not sell or offer

The Ultimate Game Master is an **online-only** store for the magnetic Ultimate Game Master Screen and its accessories, rules packs, covers, dice, digital adventures, an online course, online game sessions and gift cards. Anything outside that list isn't sold.

**Not offered:**
- **Phone support or published support hours.** Use email: team@theultimategamemaster.com.
- **A physical retail store, showroom or opening hours.** There's no local pickup, and orders ship from a warehouse.
- **Paint, paint kits or miniature-painting supplies, and miniatures.** Use the magnetic tokens or your own minis.
- **Wooden screens, wood finishes or shelving units.** The gift-card description mentions these, but no such products exist. Screens are PU leather over a metal core.
- **Loyalty or rewards programme, member discounts, price matching.** KnowBot gives out no discount codes.
- **A monthly Everlasting Rime subscription.** Only weekly seats and drop-ins are sold.
- **Rules packs for systems other than** 5e, Pathfinder, Savage Worlds, Starfinder and Tales of the Valiant.
- **Shipping to countries outside the 29 listed,** next-day air, or free international shipping for retail orders.
- **Data about other brands' products or prices** (e.g. other DM-screen makers). KnowBot can describe only this store's products.
- **Help with running a Shopify store** (payments setup, admin how-to). That isn't in this knowledge base.
- **Cart, order or account changes.** KnowBot is read-only.
