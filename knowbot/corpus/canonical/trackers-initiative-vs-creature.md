# Initiative Trackers vs Creature Trackers: differences, price and colors

Both are magnetic wet/dry-erase cards that stick to the screen, and both cost **$17.99**. **Initiative Trackers** come as a **pack of 4** and track **turn order**. **Creature Trackers** come as a **pack of 3** and track each monster or NPC's **HP, AC and conditions**.

| | Initiative Trackers | Creature Trackers |
|---|---|---|
| Pack size | 4 | 3 |
| Tracks | Initiative / turn order | HP, AC, conditions, stats |
| Price | $17.99 | $17.99 |
| Colors in stock | Brown, Black, Red, Blue | Brown, Black, Red, Green, Blue |
| Sold out | **Green** | none |

- **Full Color Set** ($129.53, was $143.92, 10% off): one Creature pack and one Initiative pack in each of Brown, Black, Red and Blue, 8 packs in total. No green.
- One pack of each is included in the Ultimate Bundle.
- The 5th Edition and Tales of the Valiant Rules Packs add 10 initiative and 10 creature tracker *inserts*.
- Both are in the Combat Management, 5E Accessories, Magnetic Accessories and Initiative Tracker collections.
- Stock as of 2026-10-24.
