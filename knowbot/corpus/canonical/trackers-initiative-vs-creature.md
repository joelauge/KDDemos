# Initiative Trackers vs Creature Trackers: differences, price and colors

Initiative Trackers come in a pack of 4 and track turn order. Creature Trackers come in a pack of 3 and track each creature's HP, AC and conditions. Each pack costs $17.99, and both are magnetic wet/dry-erase cards.

- Initiative Trackers are in stock in Brown, Black, Red and Blue. Green is sold out.
- Creature Trackers are in stock in all five colors: Brown, Black, Red, Green and Blue.
- **Full Color Set:** $129.53. It contains one Creature pack and one Initiative pack in each of Brown, Black, Red and Blue, 8 packs in all.
- One pack of each comes in the Ultimate Bundle.
- The 5th Edition and Tales of the Valiant Rules Packs each add 10 initiative inserts and 10 creature inserts.
- Stock as of 2026-10-24.
