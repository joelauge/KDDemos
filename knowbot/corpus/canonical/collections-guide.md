# Store collections: which products are in each

The store has 17 collections. Each list below is complete.

- **Combat Management (5):** Initiative Trackers, Creature Trackers, Quick-Load Panel, Magnetic Map Pack, 5th Edition Rules Pack.
- **Storage & Organization (5):** Initiative Trackers, Large Magnetic Pouch, Small Magnetic Pouch, 5th Edition Rules Pack, Blank Magnetic Charts.
- **Initiative Tracker (3):** Initiative Trackers, Creature Trackers, Blank Magnetic Charts.
- **5E Accessories (3):** Initiative Trackers, Creature Trackers, 5th Edition Rules Pack.
- **Digital Accessories (2):** Magnetic Tablet Holder, Magnetic Phone Holder.
- **Dice & Accessories (2):** Magnetic Dice Tray, UGMS Premium Dice Set with Leather Case.
- **Magnetic Covers (7):** Gothic Horror, Eldritch Horror, Sci-Fi, Epic Fantasy, Pathfinder, Tales of the Valiant, Savage Worlds.
- **Ultimate Bundles (2):** Ultimate Bundle Brown and Black.
- **Game Master Screen (1):** the Ultimate Game Master Screen.
- **Kickstarter Exclusives (10):** Dice Tray, Dice Set, Tales of the Valiant Rules Pack and the 7 covers.
- **Weekly Everlasting Rime (4):** the Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Drop-In seats.
- **Gifts (1):** the Gift Card.
- The Accessories (21) and Magnetic Accessories (12) collections are broad groupings of the accessories.
