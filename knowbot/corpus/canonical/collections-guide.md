# Store collections: which products are in each

The store has 17 collections. These are the exact members of the main ones, so list only these.

- **Combat Management (5):** Initiative Trackers (4-pack), Creature Trackers (3-pack), Quick-Load Panel, Magnetic Map Pack, 5th Edition Rules Pack.
- **Storage & Organization (5):** Initiative Trackers, Large Magnetic Pouch, Small Magnetic Pouch, 5th Edition Rules Pack, Blank Magnetic Charts.
- **Initiative Tracker (3):** Initiative Trackers, Creature Trackers, Blank Magnetic Charts.
- **5E Accessories (3):** Initiative Trackers, Creature Trackers, 5th Edition Rules Pack.
- **Digital Accessories (2):** Magnetic Tablet Holder, Magnetic Phone Holder.
- **Dice & Accessories (2):** Magnetic Dice Tray ($29.99), UGMS Premium Dice Set with Leather Case ($70).
- **Magnetic Covers (7):** Gothic Horror, Eldritch Horror, Sci-Fi, Epic Fantasy, Pathfinder, Tales of the Valiant, Savage Worlds.
- **Ultimate Bundles (2):** Ultimate Bundle Brown and Black. **Game Master Screen (1):** the screen.
- **Kickstarter Exclusives (Limited Quantity) (10):** Dice Tray, Dice Set, Tales of the Valiant Rules Pack, all 7 covers.
- **Magnetic Accessories (12)** and **Accessories (21)** are the broad accessory collections.
- **Gifts (1):** Gift Card. **Home page (1):** 5th Edition Rules Pack.
- **Empty:** Courses (0) and The Endless Rime Monthly Subscription (0). The weekly Everlasting Rime collection has 4 session products.
