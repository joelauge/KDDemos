# Ultimate Game Master Screen: price, colors, orientations and stock

The Ultimate Game Master Screen costs **$139.99 USD** in every color and orientation. It's not discounted: the compare-at price equals the price. It comes in Portrait or Landscape, and in Brown, Black, Red, Green or Blue. Six of the 10 combinations are in stock.

| Orientation | Available | Sold out |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait | Brown, Black, Green (low: 5), Blue (low: 14) | Red |
| Landscape | Brown, Black | Red, Blue, Green |

- **Red is sold out in both orientations.** Landscape is only available in Brown or Black.
- **In the box:** 4 PU-leather panels with a metal core, magnetic front and back. Also 12 blank wet/dry-erase magnetic charts in 3 sizes, 4 rivet magnets, a magnetic clasp that stores the rivets and doubles as a dice tower, and 2 cord slots. Each panel is 9 x 12 inches.
- System-agnostic: works with D&D 5e, Pathfinder, Savage Worlds, Call of Cthulhu and homebrew. Rules packs and covers are sold separately.
- Accessories (trackers, pouches, holders, Map Pack) aren't included. The Ultimate Bundle includes them.
- There's no $5, $35 or $47.99 version of the screen. Those prices belong to other items: drafts, covers and the Quick-Load Panel.
- Stock reflects store data as of 2026-10-24.
