# Free shipping: the $100 US threshold and what qualifies

Shipping is free for US orders of $100.00 or more. US orders under $100 pay a UPS or USPS rate calculated at checkout.

- The free-shipping rate applies only to delivery addresses in the United States.
- US orders under $100 are charged the UPS or USPS rate the customer picks at checkout.
- Orders shipped outside the US pay a UPS rate calculated at checkout. Regular customers get no free shipping outside the US.
- Free shipping applies automatically at checkout. No code is needed.
- Approved wholesale customers get free shipping to all countries on orders of $500 or more.
