# Free shipping: the $100 US threshold and what qualifies

US orders ship free when the order total is **$100.00 or more**. Below $100, US shipping is charged at UPS or USPS rates calculated at checkout. There is no free shipping outside the US for regular customers.

- Checkout applies the "FREE SHIPPING" rate ($0.00) in the United States zone only when the order total is at least $100.00 USD.
- Under $100, the customer chooses a calculated UPS or USPS rate at checkout.
- International orders never get the free rate. Rates are calculated by UPS at checkout.
- Free shipping is a shipping rate, not a discount code. Nothing needs to be entered.
- Approved wholesale customers get free shipping to all countries on orders of $500+ (see the wholesale card).

**Conflicting store text.** The shipping policy page says "FREE shipping to anywhere in the United States" with no minimum (policies/shipping.md). The FAQ says "free on US orders over $99" (pages/avada-faqs.md). Neither matches checkout. The live Shopify delivery profile sets the threshold at $100.00 or more (operations/shipping.md), and that is what customers are actually charged by.
