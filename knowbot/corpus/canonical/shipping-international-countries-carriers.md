# Shipping outside the US: countries, carriers and costs

The store ships to 29 countries. Shipping outside the US is charged at a UPS rate calculated at checkout.

- **Countries:** United States, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom.
- Other countries, for example Brazil, India or South Africa, are not available at checkout.
- **International carrier:** UPS (Ground, Ground Saver, Standard, Worldwide Expedited, Worldwide Express, Worldwide Express Plus, Worldwide Saver).
- Regular customers get no flat rate and no free shipping outside the US.
- Prices are in USD. Canada and Mexico are priced in CAD.
- Taxes are added at checkout, and shipping is taxed.
- If an order outside the US has to be split, extra shipping charges may apply.
