# Shipping outside the US: countries, carriers and costs

Yes, the store ships internationally, to 28 countries outside the US (29 including the US). International shipping is never free or flat-rate. UPS calculates it at checkout.

- **Countries served:** Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, plus the United States. Countries not on this list are not served.
- **International services (UPS):** Ground, Ground Saver, Standard, Worldwide Expedited, Worldwide Express, Worldwide Express Plus, Worldwide Saver.
- **US services:** UPS (Ground, Ground Saver, Standard, 2nd Day Air, 3 Day Select, Worldwide options) and USPS (First Class Mail, Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express). Next Day Air and USPS Media Mail are not offered.
- **Currency:** prices are in USD. The Canada and North America markets are priced in CAD.
- **Taxes:** prices don't include tax. Tax is calculated at checkout, and shipping is taxed. Import duties are not described anywhere in store data.
- If an international order has to be split, extra shipping charges may apply (policies/shipping.md).

The policy page says shipping elsewhere is "calculated by weight". In practice it is a UPS carrier-calculated rate shown at checkout (operations/shipping.md).
