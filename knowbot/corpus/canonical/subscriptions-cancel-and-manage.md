# Subscriptions: what is sold by subscription and how to cancel

You can cancel or change a subscription **at any time** yourself. Use the link in your order confirmation email to open your order and manage the subscription there. You don't need to email the store first.

- **What's sold as a subscription:** seats in the weekly online D&D campaign *The Everlasting Rime*, on Monday, Wednesday or Thursday night. Each is $50.00 and billed weekly, and checkout requires a subscription plan.
- **Drop-In** ($50.00) is a one-off purchase, not a subscription. It's for joining a session other than your usual one. Confirm availability before buying.
- There's no monthly subscription for sale. The "Endless Rime (Monthly Subscription)" collection has no products.
- Physical products (screens, accessories) aren't sold by subscription.
- Some subscriptions may auto-renew. Cancel to stop renewal.
- **Pre-orders:** a partly paid pre-order can be cancelled before it's fulfilled. After fulfillment, request a refund under the returns policy instead.
- For refunds on subscription charges, the policy refers you to the returns policy. You can also email team@theultimategamemaster.com.
