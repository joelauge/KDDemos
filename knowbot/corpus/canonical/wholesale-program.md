# Wholesale and retail partners: tiers, minimums and free shipping

Yes, the store runs a wholesale programme for retail partners. Approved wholesale accounts get 25%, 40% or 50% off, plus free shipping to any country, depending on order size.

| Tier | Discount | Minimum order |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 25% off the whole order | $500.00 |
| Tier 2 | 40% off the whole order | $1,000.00 |
| Tier 3 | 50% off the whole order | $1,500.00 |

- Every tier includes free shipping to all countries.
- The discounts apply only to accounts the store has approved for a tier.
- To join, go to the Wholesale Partner Portal (/pages/wholesale) or email team@theultimategamemaster.com.
