# Wholesale and retail partners: tiers, minimums and free shipping

Yes, the store has a wholesale programme for retail partners. Approved wholesale customers get automatic tiered discounts and free shipping to all countries.

| Tier | Discount | Minimum order | Shipping |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 25% off entire order | $500.00 | Free, all countries |
| Tier 2 | 40% off entire order | $1,000.00 | Free, all countries |
| Tier 3 | 50% off entire order | $1,500.00 | Free, all countries |

- The discounts apply only to customers approved for a wholesale tier. They don't apply to regular retail customers, whatever the order size.
- Apply or log in through the Wholesale Partner Portal page (/pages/wholesale). The page has no further application details. Email team@theultimategamemaster.com with questions.
- All tiers have been active since 2026-06-20, with no end date.

**Conflicting store text.** The Terms of Service say purchases must be "for your own personal or household use and not for commercial resale" (policies/terms.md). That conflicts with the wholesale programme the FAQ and discounts describe (pages/avada-faqs.md, operations/promotions.md). The wholesale tiers are live in checkout, so treat resale as allowed for approved wholesale partners, and the Terms clause as applying to retail purchases.
