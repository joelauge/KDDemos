# Ship times: when orders leave the warehouse and delivery estimates

The store aims to ship orders within 2 business days. Delivery time after that depends on the UPS or USPS service chosen at checkout.

- Physical orders ship from the 2Fold Supply Warehouse in Lee's Summit, Missouri, USA.
- Delivery dates are estimates and are not guaranteed.
- If an item is missing from the warehouse, the store contacts the customer to arrange a solution.
- Once the parcel is with the carrier, risk of loss passes to the buyer.
- Digital downloads are available immediately after purchase. Online sessions and the online course do not ship.
- Local pickup is not available.
