# Ship times: when orders leave the warehouse and delivery estimates

The official shipping policy promises a best effort to ship within **2 business days** of the order. The store doesn't publish guaranteed delivery dates, and all delivery times are estimates.

- Physical orders ship from the 2Fold Supply Warehouse in Lee's Summit, Missouri, USA.
- Transit time depends on the UPS/USPS service chosen at checkout. The store publishes no transit-day figures.
- If items are missing from the warehouse, the store contacts the customer to arrange a solution. Outside the US, a split order may carry extra shipping charges.
- The Terms of Service say the store isn't liable for carrier or customs delays, and that risk of loss passes to the buyer once the parcel is handed to the carrier (policies/terms.md).
- Digital downloads are delivered immediately after purchase. Online game sessions and the course don't ship.
- Local pickup is not available.

**Conflicting store text.** The FAQ says "In-stock orders ship within 24 hours" (pages/avada-faqs.md). The shipping policy says "within 2 business days" (policies/shipping.md). Quote the policy's 2 business days as the commitment. The 24-hour figure is marketing copy.
