# Contacting the store: email only, no phone line or published hours

Customer service is by **email: team@theultimategamemaster.com**. The store publishes no customer-service phone number, no live chat line and no support hours.

- Use the same email for returns, damaged or wrong items, refund follow-ups, order questions, privacy requests and Terms questions.
- **Business address** (contact policy, also the returns address): 13665 E 42 Terr S, Ste F, Independence, Missouri 64055, USA. Don't send returns without an approved request.
- The FAQ links to a "Get in touch" contact page (/pages/contact). Its page body is empty in store data, so it adds no phone number or hours.
- The store is online-only. There's no retail shop to visit, and local pickup isn't offered. Physical orders ship from a third-party warehouse in Lee's Summit, MO.
- Store time zone: America/Chicago (Central). This is a setting, not published support hours.

**Conflicting store text.** The privacy policy says "please call or email us" and lists a Cheyenne, WY mailing address for The Ultimate Game Master, LLC (policies/privacy.md). No phone number is published anywhere customer-facing, so never give one. Never invent a phone number or opening hours.
