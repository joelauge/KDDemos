# Contacting the store: email only, no phone line or published hours

The way to contact the store is email: team@theultimategamemaster.com. The store has no published customer-service phone number and no published support hours.

- Email covers order questions, returns, damaged or wrong items, refund follow-ups and privacy requests.
- **Business and returns address:** 13665 E 42 Terr S, Ste F, Independence, Missouri 64055, USA. Send returns there only after the store approves them by email.
- The store sells online only. It has no retail shop, and local pickup is not available.
- Physical orders ship from a warehouse in Lee's Summit, Missouri.
