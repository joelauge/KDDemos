# Returns and refunds: the 30-day window, conditions and how to start

Returns are accepted within 30 days of receiving the item. After 30 days, a return is not accepted. To start a return, email team@theultimategamemaster.com.

- **Condition:** the item must be unused and as received, with tags, in its original packaging, with a receipt or proof of purchase.
- **Process:** email first. If the store approves the return, it sends a return label and instructions. Items sent back without an approved request are not accepted.
- **Not returnable:** sale items and gift cards.
- **Damaged, defective or wrong items:** contact the store as soon as the order arrives.
- **Exchanges:** return the item, then place a new order for the one you want.
- **Refunds:** approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days. If 15 business days pass after approval, email the store.
- There are no restocking fees.
- Orders shipped into the EU also have a 14-day right to cancel.

Resumen en español: las devoluciones se aceptan dentro de los 30 días posteriores a la recepción; después de 30 días no se aceptan. Escribe a team@theultimategamemaster.com. El artículo debe estar sin usar y en su embalaje original. En la Unión Europea hay además 14 días de desistimiento.
