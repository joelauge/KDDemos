# Returns and refunds: the 30-day window, conditions and how to start

You have **30 days after receiving an item** to request a return. To start one, email team@theultimategamemaster.com. There is no 60- or 90-day window.

- **Condition:** same as received, unused, with tags, in original packaging, with a receipt or proof of purchase.
- **How:** email first. If the return is accepted, the store sends a return label and instructions. Items sent back without an approved request are not accepted. The returns address is 13665 E 42 Terr S, Ste F, Independence, Missouri 64055, USA.
- **Not returnable:** sale items and gift cards. Some items to some areas can't be returned, and not every request is accepted.
- **Damaged, defective or wrong item:** contact the store immediately on receipt.
- **Exchanges:** there are no direct exchanges. Return the item, then place a new order.
- **Refund timing:** refunds go to the original payment method within 10 business days of approval. Email the store if 15 business days pass with no refund.
- No restocking fees (policies/shipping.md).
- EU shipments also get a 14-day cancellation right (see the EU card).

**Conflicting store text.** The FAQ calls it a "30-night risk-free return… full refund, no questions" (pages/avada-faqs.md). The operative policy is the refund policy's 30 days from receipt, with conditions and approval (policies/refund.md).

Resumen en español: 30 días desde la recepción para solicitar una devolución por correo a team@theultimategamemaster.com. El artículo debe estar sin usar y en su embalaje original. En la UE hay 14 días de desistimiento.
