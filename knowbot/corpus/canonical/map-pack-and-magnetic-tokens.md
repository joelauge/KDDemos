# Magnetic Map Pack and Magnetic Tokens: what each includes

The **Magnetic Map Pack** ($54.99, in stock) is a set of dry-erase magnetic battle maps. It does **not** come with miniatures or tokens. Tokens are a separate product: the **Set of 15 Magnetic Tokens** ($17.00).

**Magnetic Map Pack**
- Dry-erase surface for drawing terrain and marking positions, with a magnetic grid for miniature positioning.
- Use it attached to the screen or detached on the table. The maps are reusable.
- The store doesn't list the number of maps or their size.
- Included in the Ultimate Bundle. Also in the Combat Management collection.
- 56 in stock.

**Set of 15 Magnetic Tokens (for Map Pack)**
- 15 flat magnetic tokens that grip the Map Pack's 1-inch grid. Use them in place of minis to mark heroes and monsters.
- $17.00. Only **2 left**, and the product is active but not published on the online store.
- Not included in the Map Pack or the Ultimate Bundle.

- The store sells no miniatures and no miniature-painting supplies.
- Stock as of 2026-10-24.
