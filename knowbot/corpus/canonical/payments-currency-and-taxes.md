# Paying: currencies, accepted wallets, taxes and order acceptance

Prices are in **US dollars**. Canada and Mexico see CAD pricing. Taxes aren't included in listed prices and are added at checkout. Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay are supported as express wallets.

- **Currencies:** USD (base) and CAD. The United States and International markets price in USD. The Canada and North America (Canada, Mexico) markets price in CAD. No other local currencies are offered.
- **Taxes:** calculated at checkout. Shipping is taxed. Posted prices exclude taxes, shipping and any customs or import charges (Terms of Service).
- **Wallets:** Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Store data doesn't list the full set of card brands or other payment methods.
- **Order acceptance:** an order is an offer. It's accepted only once the store confirms it, after payment is processed. The store may decline orders, and may not be able to cancel an order after acceptance.
- **Price changes:** the price charged is the price in effect when the order is placed, as shown in the confirmation email.
