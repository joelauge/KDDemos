# Paying: currencies, accepted wallets, taxes and order acceptance

Prices are in US dollars, and customers in Canada and Mexico pay in Canadian dollars. Taxes are added at checkout. Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted.

- The store uses two currencies: USD and CAD.
- Listed prices don't include tax, shipping, or customs and import charges.
- Shipping is taxed.
- **Express wallets:** Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
- An order is accepted once the store confirms it after payment. The store may decline orders.
- You pay the price in effect when you place the order, as shown in your confirmation email.
