# Privacy: what the privacy policy says about selling or sharing data

The store's privacy policy (Shopify's standard policy text) includes the legal disclosure required by US state privacy laws: personal information may be "sold" or "shared" for advertising and marketing, "as those terms are defined in applicable law".

- **What the legal terms mean:** under laws such as California's CCPA, "sale" and "sharing" include letting advertising partners' cookies and pixels collect browsing data to show targeted ads on other websites. They are legal definitions in the policy text, not a statement that customer lists are sold.
- **The opt-out:** customers can opt out of any "sale", "sharing" or targeted advertising on the store's "Your Privacy Choices" page. A Global Privacy Control browser signal is honoured as an opt-out automatically.
- **Sensitive personal information** is used only with consent.
- **Service providers** that help run the store (payment processing, fulfillment and shipping, analytics, customer support) receive the data they need to do that work.
- **Questions:** for specifics about the store's data practices, email team@theultimategamemaster.com.
