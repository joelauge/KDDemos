# Privacy: does the store sell or share personal data with advertisers?

**Yes.** The privacy policy states that the store has "sold" and "shared" personal information (as those terms are defined in applicable law) over the preceding 12 months for advertising and marketing. It also discloses information to business and marketing partners. Never tell a customer the store does not sell or share their data.

- **Categories sold or shared** with business and marketing partners: identifiers (name, email, phone number), commercial information (purchase records) and usage data.
- Other disclosures go to vendors such as payment processors, fulfillment and shipping partners, analytics and customer support providers, and to affiliates.
- The "Your Privacy Choices" page says it may share personal information "with third parties, including advertising partners" to show more relevant ads on other websites.
- Cookies and tracking pixels are used, including by third parties for advertising.
- Sensitive personal information isn't used or disclosed without consent. The store has no actual knowledge of selling or sharing data of anyone under 16.
- You can opt out (see the privacy rights card). Global Privacy Control signals are honoured as an opt-out.

The Terms of Service don't promise otherwise. Answer data-sharing questions from policies/privacy.md.
