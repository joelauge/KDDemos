# EU customers: the 14-day right to cancel or return

Yes. Orders shipped into the European Union get an extra right: you can cancel or return the order **within 14 days, for any reason and without giving a justification**. This applies on top of the normal 30-day return policy.

- The refund policy calls this the "European Union 14 day cooling off period" and says it applies "notwithstanding" the other return rules.
- The same condition rules still apply: unused, with tags, in original packaging, with a receipt or proof of purchase.
- To use it, email team@theultimategamemaster.com, as for any return.
- EU countries the store ships to: Austria, Belgium, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Sweden. Customers in Germany, France and so on qualify.
- The UK, Switzerland and Norway are served but aren't EU members. The policy grants the 14-day right only for shipments "into the European Union".
- The policy doesn't say who pays return shipping for EU withdrawals.

Resumen en español: si el pedido se envía a la Unión Europea, tienes 14 días para cancelar o devolver sin justificación, además de la política general de 30 días.
