# EU customers: the 14-day right to cancel or return

Yes. Orders shipped into the European Union can be cancelled or returned within 14 days, for any reason and without giving a justification. This applies on top of the store's standard 30-day return policy.

- The item must be unused and as received, with tags, in its original packaging, with a receipt or proof of purchase.
- To use this right, email team@theultimategamemaster.com.
- **EU countries the store ships to:** Austria, Belgium, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Sweden.
- The 14-day right covers shipments into the EU. The UK, Switzerland and Norway are outside the EU and follow the standard 30-day policy.

Resumen en español: si el pedido se envía a la Unión Europea, tienes 14 días para cancelar o devolver sin justificación, además de la política general de 30 días.
