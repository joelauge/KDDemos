# Privacy rights: opting out, deleting data and who to contact

To opt out of the sale or sharing of your data, use the "Your Privacy Choices" page or browse with Global Privacy Control turned on. Send any other privacy request to team@theultimategamemaster.com.

- **Global Privacy Control:** counts as an opt-out for that browser or device, and for the matching account if one exists.
- **Other rights:** depending on where you live, you can ask the store to give you a copy of your data, delete or correct it, transfer it, or restrict how it's used. You can also withdraw consent, and appeal a refusal by replying to the denial.
- **Marketing emails:** unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. Order and account emails continue.
- The store may verify your identity first. An authorised agent can make requests with proof.
- Customers in the EEA can complain to their local data protection authority.
- The privacy policy was last updated on June 30, 2026.
