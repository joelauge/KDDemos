# Privacy rights: opting out, deleting data and who to contact

Depending on where you live, you may be able to opt out of the "sale", "sharing" or targeted-advertising use of your data, and to access, delete, correct or port it. Requests go to team@theultimategamemaster.com.

- **Opt-out of sale/sharing/targeted ads:** use the "Your Privacy Choices" page, or browse with Global Privacy Control enabled. GPC is treated as an opt-out for that browser or device, and for the linked account if one can be matched. Other "Do Not Track" signals are not recognised.
- **Other rights:** access/know, delete, correct, portability, restrict processing, withdraw consent and appeal a refusal (reply to the denial). Rights vary by location and aren't absolute.
- **Marketing emails:** unsubscribe at any time with the link in any email. Order and account emails still arrive.
- The store may verify your identity first. An authorised agent can make requests with proof.
- EEA users can complain to their local data protection authority.
- Policy last updated: June 30, 2026.
