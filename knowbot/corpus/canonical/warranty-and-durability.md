# Warranty: what the store's terms say about warranties and durability

No. The store publishes no product warranty, and its Terms of Service include warranty disclaimers.

- The Terms of Service say: "We do not warrant that the appearance or quality of any products or services purchased by you will meet your expectations or be the same as depicted or rendered in our online stores."
- "Lifetime durability" on the screen comparison page describes the screen's construction (premium PU leather and a metal core). It is not a warranty.
- Defective, damaged or wrong items: inspect the order on arrival and contact the store immediately at team@theultimategamemaster.com so the issue can be evaluated and made right.
- Returns are accepted within 30 days of receiving the item (see the returns card).
