## Description

# Twisted Hand of Fate — A Complete 6-Part 5E Campaign

### *Part of the Seeds of Chaos saga*

**A sleepy tavern. A girl who walks in her sleep. A murder no one saw coming.**

In the soot-stained town of Blackwell, the Shattered Shield tavern is a rare beacon of warmth — until a barmaid drops dead and the owner's adopted daughter, Violet, vanishes into the night. What begins as a single grim evening unravels into a descent through haunted guild halls, flooded sewers, a fog-choked marsh, and the crooked tower of a night hag who has been pulling Violet's strings since the day she was born.

*Twisted Hand of Fate* is a fully-realized, six-chapter adventure that takes a brand-new party from their first tankard of ale to a desperate confrontation with one of the Feywild's cruelest predators.

## The Six Chapters

1. **The Shattered Shield Tavern** — Meet the town, work the room, and witness the murder that sets everything in motion.
2. **Brigand's Corridor** — Follow Violet's trail past the Maranam bandits into a wine cellar and the sealed ruins of an ancient guild hall.
3. **When the Night Beckons** — Brave the haunted halls and the dripping sewers beneath Blackwell.
4. **Into the Black Marshes** — Track your quarry through a swamp that has been growing for reasons no one understands.
5. **The Streets of Blackwell** — Race through town by night, from the guard station to the Butter Belle Bakery, as the hag's plan accelerates.
6. **The Twisted Tower** — Climb to the hag's lair, face the Fell-Kin and the Lady who commands them, and fight to free Violet before the final ritual is complete.
