## Description

Purchase this for a seat in our Wednesday night Everlasting Rime weekly campaign.

North of the *Spine of the World* and west of the towering *Reghed Glacier* is a frigid expanse few dare to explore, let alone inhabit. This icy land of windswept tundra recently became locked in a perpetual, dark winter without reprieve. *Auril the Frostmaiden,* the divine embodiment of winter's fury, is suspected to have cast a terrible spell over *Icewind Dale*, to the detriment of most of its denizens.

Will you be instrumental in bringing an end to the Everlasting Rime? Venture into *Icewind Dale* and find out!

**Bio:** Jason is a professional Dungeon Master, acclaimed miniature painter and the President & Founder of RealmSmith. He's had the honor of having Matt Mercer, Matthew Lillard, Christian Navarro, Deborah Ann Woll, Sean Gunn, and many more at his table. He's best known as the DM for RealmSmith's Curse of Strahd campaign 'Into the Mist', 'Faster, Purple Worm, Kill! Kill!', and has been a guest DM at D&D in a Castle and D3atSea.
