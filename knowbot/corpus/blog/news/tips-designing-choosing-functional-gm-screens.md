## What Is a GM Screen and Why Is It a Must-Have for D&D and TTRPG Play?

A **GM screen** (or **Dungeon Master screen**) is a foldable, multi-panel barrier placed between the GM and the players during a session. For **Dungeons & Dragons 5e**, it’s invaluable: it hides story secrets, tracks initiative, and contains **5e rules**, spell effects, and encounter stats—all at a glance.

If you’ve ever searched for "D&D 5e combat tracker" or “D&D reference screen,” this is what you’re looking for.

### D&D 5e GM Screen: Best Use Cases and Layout Tips

- A **D&D 5e GM screen** works best when organized like this:
- Center: **Combat actions and conditions**
- Left panel: **Spellcasting and saving throws**
- Right panel: **NPC generator and environment effects**
- Behind the screen: Maps, tokens, and monster stats
- Make sure your panels are aligned with the **official D&D 5e SRD rules** for maximum utility.
