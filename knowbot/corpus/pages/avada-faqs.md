## Product & Compatibility

### Which game systems does it work with?

Any of them. The Ultimate Game Master Screen is system-agnostic — D&D 5e, Pathfinder, Savage Worlds, Call of Cthulhu, and any homebrew. Add a system-specific Rules Pack for printed magnetic charts.

### Portrait or landscape — which should I pick?

Both cost the same. Portrait gives you taller coverage and a bigger wall of notes; landscape keeps a lower profile for better sightlines across the table. The magnetic panels work either way.

### What comes in the box?

Your core screen includes the 4-panel magnetic screen, 12 blank magnetic charts, 4 rivet magnets, and the magnetic clasp that doubles as a dice tower.

### Are accessories included?

The core screen includes the charts, rivets, and clasp. Trackers, pouches, holders, and the Magnetic Map Pack are sold separately — or grab the [Ultimate Bundle](https://theultimategamemaster.com/products/ultimate-bundle) to get the screen plus nine essentials and save.

### How do the magnetic accessories attach?

Every panel has a steel core, so accessories snap on anywhere and hold firm through the most chaotic combat. Rearrange your whole command center in seconds — no clips, no clutter.
