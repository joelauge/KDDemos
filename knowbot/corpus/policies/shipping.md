# Shipping policy — The Ultimate Game Master

**FREE SHIPPING TO THE UNITED STATES**
We off FREE shipping to anywhere in the United States. Everywhere else, shipping is calculated by weight at checkout.

**SHIPPING EVERYWHERE ELSE**
We offer weight-based shipping to anywhere else in the world. Shipping is calculated by weight and is clearly identified on the checkout page.

**SHIPPING TIMES**
We do our absolute best to ship within 2 business days of the original order. If for some reason, products are missing from the warehouse, and we need to modify the order for whatever reason, we will contact your directly to inform you and coordinate an appropriate shipping solution for everyone. This may include extra shipping charges if an order is required to be split up (only outside of the United States.)

**RESTOCKING FEES**There are no restocking fees. If you are unsatisfied with your product, simply send it back to us in it's original packaging and we will restock the items.
