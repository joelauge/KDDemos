# Shipping zones, carriers and rates — The Ultimate Game Master

This describes the store's shipping configuration in Shopify (delivery profile "General profile", which covers all
120 product variants). For the customer-facing shipping policy text see `policies/shipping.md`.

## Where the store ships

The store ships to 29 countries: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, United States.

## United States zone

- **FREE SHIPPING** — $0.00 on orders with a total price of **$100.00 or more** (USD).
- **UPS (calculated rates):** UPS® Ground, UPS® Ground Saver, UPS® Standard, UPS 2nd Day Air®, UPS 3 Day Select®,
  UPS Worldwide Expedited®, UPS Worldwide Express®, UPS Worldwide Express Plus®, UPS Worldwide Saver®.
- **USPS (calculated rates):** First Class Mail, Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express.
- Not offered: UPS Next Day Air®, UPS Next Day Air® Early, UPS Next Day Air Saver®, UPS 2nd Day Air A.M.®, USPS Media Mail.

## "All International" zone

Countries: Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Malaysia, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, Mexico.

- **UPS (calculated rates):** UPS® Ground, UPS® Ground Saver, UPS® Standard, UPS Worldwide Expedited®,
  UPS Worldwide Express®, UPS Worldwide Express Plus®, UPS Worldwide Saver®.
- No flat or free-shipping rate is configured for international orders; rates are calculated by UPS at checkout.

## Notes

- Shipping rates are carrier-calculated at checkout (UPS and USPS) except the US free-shipping rate.
- Shipping is taxed (store setting); prices do not include tax.
- A secondary delivery profile named "Canada Shipping" exists but currently has no products assigned.
